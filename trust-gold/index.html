Questions

Things people ask first.

What should I bring to sell gold?

Your gold items and a valid photo ID such as Aadhaar. If any item has a purchase bill, bring that too — it is helpful but not always required.

How is my gold tested?

We check purity and weight in front of you and show you the reading. Testing is free and you are under no obligation to sell.

How is the price decided?

Price = weight × purity × the day's gold rate. We show you each part of the calculation so you can see exactly how the offer is made.

How does pledged gold release work?

You share your pledge details, we verify the outstanding amount, the loan is cleared and your gold is brought back. It is then weighed and tested, and the balance is settled with you.

How do I get paid?

Once you accept the offer, payment is made without delay by bank transfer or other modes permitted by law. Ask us for the options available.

Can I speak in Telugu?

Yes. You can talk to us, and read this website, in Telugu or English.
