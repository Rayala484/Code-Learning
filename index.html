FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can join these courses?

Anyone interested in a career in the gold and jewellery sector — students, job seekers, bank & NBFC staff, jewellers and entrepreneurs. No prior experience is required.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. On successful completion of your program and assessment, you receive a GoldXpert Academy certificate.

Are the classes practical?

Absolutely. Our training is hands-on — you’ll work with real testing tools and samples during lab sessions.

What are the fees and batch timings?

Fees and timings vary by program. Call or WhatsApp us on +91 85003 86916 and we’ll share the latest details.

What careers can I pursue?

Gold appraiser, gold-loan officer, gold auditor, hallmarking technician, valuer, pawn-broking professional, or start your own business.
