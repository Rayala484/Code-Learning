Our Mission

Creating confident, skilled & responsible gold professionals

Our mission is to provide high-quality, practical and industry-relevant training that empowers individuals to succeed in the gold, jewellery and precious metals sector. We deliver accurate knowledge, ethical guidance and hands-on expertise through structured courses, real-time workshops and technology-enabled learning.

By combining traditional craftsmanship with modern testing, valuation and auditing techniques, we support gold workers, appraisers, craftsmen, entrepreneurs and students — enhancing their career opportunities and contributing to the growth, transparency and professionalism of the gold industry.

FAQ

Questions, answered

I have no experience. Can I join?

Yes. Our courses start from the basics. Whether you want to make gold ornaments, start manufacturing, or become an appraiser, we train you step by step.

Will I work with real gold and tools?

Yes. Training is hands-on — you practise in real-time workshops with professional tools, testing equipment and samples.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. On successful completion of your course and assessment, you receive a GoldXpert Skill Development Academy certificate.

What are the fees and batch timings?

See the Fees & Batches section above. For the latest fee and next batch date, call or WhatsApp +91 85003 86916 (Mon – Sat, 10 AM – 7 PM).

Can you help me start my own gold business?

Yes. Our manufacturing and business-focused programs cover workshop setup, quality control, valuation and ethical trade practices to help entrepreneurs get started.

What careers can I pursue?

Goldsmith / ornament maker, jewellery manufacturer, gold appraiser, gold-loan officer, gold auditor, hallmarking technician, valuer, pawn-broking professional — or your own business.
