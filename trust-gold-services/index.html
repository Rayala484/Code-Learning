Ask on WhatsApp
What is your processing fee?

Only 1% of the total value of your gold. There are no hidden charges — every deduction is explained before you agree.

Which types of gold do you buy?

All three: BIS hallmarked 22K jewellery, jewellery with only the 916 mark, and jewellery with other marks or no marks. Purity is tested in your presence.

Can you release gold pledged with any bank or NBFC?

We help release gold pledged with banks and NBFCs. Share your lender's name and loan details with us and we will guide you through the process.

What documents do I need?

Aadhaar card, PAN card, house verification, gold bills (if available) and your bank details. For bank or NBFC release, 2 security cheques are also required.

Will my gold be melted?

Only if you ask for it. If you prefer, we buy your gold directly without melting. When melting is done, it happens in your presence.

How is the value calculated?

Net gold weight × purity × the day's gold rate, minus the 1% processing fee (and the loan amount, for pledged gold). We show you each figure before proceeding.
